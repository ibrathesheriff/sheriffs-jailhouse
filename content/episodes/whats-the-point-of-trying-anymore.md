## What can you do to ensure your product can't be Sherlocked?
You need to find a safe habour:
+ **1Password**: the trust safe harbor. Has fought of password managers by Apple and Google by people trusting them more.
+ **Kody**: what the labs won't build. A way to integratte competitors into a tool. They want to support their own ecosystem.
+ **Dropbox**: a feature, not a product. iCloud just feels like a second-class citizen in other environments.
+ **Raycast's extension**: Community. Having a community that can build their own features and shape the product based on their needs.
+ **Waze**: safe habour of proprietary data. Having Users add data daily that makes the app better and better. Competitors will struggle to catch up because they do not have that unique dataset.
+ *Nathan Barry and Kit**: Audience. Writing and teaching in public him build an audience. He then built an email software that he sold to that audience. Though that's Maichimp's market.
+ **Abridge**: Domain depth. Go deep on a specific corner more than a bigger would bother to go. 
+ Niches too small for giants. A market worth a few million dollars a year won't be worth it for big players. But why aren't there in the market:
    - Disincentivised
    - Unsuited
    - Unwelcome
    - Or, it's just an empty harbour (no buyers).

## The Test
+ Could a giant build it tomorrow, with no downside?
+ Would they want to?
+ Avoid empty harbours.
