# Your Website Won't Matter in 2027. Prepare Now.
Some research says that 43% of buyers are using an AI assistant to research something before they buy.

1 in 4 say AI is their main source for recommendations. Ahead of going to a company's website.

53% of AI users say that they mainly use it compare options and narrow them down.
But 85% of people who use AI still double what it told them somewhere else.

## Where is the AI getting its answers from?
+ YouTube - more than anywhere else.
+ Reddit used to be the one it cited the most.

YouTube is now the single most cited source in Google's AI answers. Almost 30% of Google's AI answers have a YouTube video in them.

The AI can tell everything you say in that video. AI can get:
+ what you specialise in
+ what area you work in
By the end of the day, people work with whoever feels safest and most familiar. A website is never going to do that, the way a video can.

Marcus Sheridan (author of [They Ask, You Answer](https://marcussheridan.com/they-ask-you-answer/)) prediced that your YouTube channel will be more important than your website.

similarweb tracked 1.3 billion clicks out of AI chatbots in a single month and the number one place they went to next was YouTube. Higher than Facebook, Reddit and Wikipedia compared!

## Making a website and copy is too easy now because of AI
When everyone's site is great then what's the differentiator?

The one thing AI can't fake is *you*.

Your website is down being to the last part in the sales process, the first part is your YouTube channel. For this to work you need 2 kinds of videos:
+ **The logistical questions**: answer what people are already asking about hiring somebody like you.
	- Questions you tend to get on sales calls and email inbox.
	- Questions they ask before they decide who they want to go with.
	- Answer one question per video.
	- Biggest question you want to answer: pricing.
	- Comparison video: compare to other options/competitors.
	- Process video: what to to expect when working with you, or your workflow/process.
	- Timeline video: how long before I can see results.
	- How to choose video: questions you should ask before hiring person/company X.
	- Make sure these videos are for people first not machines.
+ **Claim jumpers**: call out what you specialise in and who you do your best work for.
	- Case studies: stories of exact right client. Who they were, what you did together and what changed for them.
	- Topic choices: what you choose to make videos about tells people who you serve.
	- Mentioning the people you work with: I was working with person X and we worked on Y.
