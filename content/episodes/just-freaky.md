Have you ever watched a day-time horror like Midsommar,

![Midsommar movie poster](/img/episodes/horror/midsommar-poster-.jpg)

Or, a horror slash comedy movie like The Menu.

![The Menu movie poster](/img/episodes/horror/the-menu.jpeg)

These movies tend to be set in a well lit environment, or a comfortable place. It's not the a creepy town or woods, in fact the characters think they are there to enjoy and celebrate. There appear to be no red flags.

But for the movie viewer there's something off about the whole thing. There's a discomfort you feel as you watch the movie. You know someone is just about to die but you are not yet sure how.

## Months ago?
Today Claude (*Sonnet 5 - Medium*) hit me with this one when chatting about a `bcrypt` issue:

![Bug we hit and fixed months ago](/img/episodes/ai/we-fixed-months-ago.png)

It clearly says 20 July (of course 20 July this year). What's happening here? Am I just about to be in a day-time horror?

Is this hallucination? Is this from someone else's chat? I don't know, it's such a small thing but why does it leave me in a state of discomfort?
